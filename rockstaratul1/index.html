Adding the Repository to Sileo

1. Open the Sileo app from your home screen.

2. Tap the “Sources” tab at the bottom.

3. Tap the “+” button in the top right corner to add a new source.

4. Paste the following URL into the input box and tap “Add Source”:

https://apt.tinyapps.cn/rockstaratul1

5. Wait for the source to refresh, and you can then browse and install its contents.

Adding the Repository to Cydia

1. Open the Cydia app from your home screen.

2. Tap the “Sources” tab at the bottom.

3. Tap “Edit” in the top right corner, then tap “Add”.

4. Paste the following URL into the input box and tap “Add Source”:

https://apt.tinyapps.cn/rockstaratul1

5. Wait for the source to refresh, and you can then browse and install its contents.
